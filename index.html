Founded in 2010, Pointhill is a full-service interior design studio dedicated to transforming ordinary spaces into extraordinary experiences. Our approach is rooted in listening — understanding how you live, work, and dream.

Every project is a collaboration. We blend curated materials, bespoke furnishings, and thoughtful spatial planning to create interiors that feel both aspirational and deeply personal.

Our Services

Comprehensive design solutions tailored to residential, commercial, and hospitality spaces.

Residential Design

From city apartments to country estates, we create homes that reflect your lifestyle with warmth, sophistication, and enduring style.

Commercial Spaces

Strategic workplace design that elevates brand identity, boosts productivity, and creates memorable experiences for clients and employees.

Styling & Staging
